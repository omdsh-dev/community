# Contributing

[Chinese](CONTRIBUTING.zh-CN.md) | English

You do not need organization membership to contribute.

- Questions and ideas: Discussions.
- Defined work: issues labeled `good first issue` or `help wanted`.
- Code/docs: pull requests in the relevant project.
- Community decisions: Community Proposal form.
- Upstream breakage: Compatibility Report form.
- Sensitive matters: private routes in `SECURITY.md` or `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.

## Claiming work

Comment `/claim`; a claim normally lasts seven days. Post an update to extend it or `/unclaim` to release it. Inactivity is not punished, but maintainers may release an inactive claim.

Actionable issues should state the problem, acceptance criteria, affected project/files, difficulty, contact, and known safety or compatibility considerations.

## Pull requests

Keep a PR focused, explain impact and validation, and link the issue or proposal. Project maintainers own technical decisions in their repositories; cross-project policy also links a community proposal.

AI assistance is welcome when contributors review the result, follow licenses, disclose material use when provenance/review is affected, and remain responsible. Never include secrets, private data, or unlicensed material.

## Access and language

Access follows responsibility, not status. Trial advancement uses public contribution evidence, one non-conflicted sponsor, least privilege, and a 90-day review.

English is the default documentation language. Simplified Chinese lives in separate `.zh-CN.md` files; do not mix both languages in one document. High-impact documents maintain both versions. Material translation differences pause the decision until reconciled; neither version silently overrides the other during the trial.
